# Product Designer

## STATUS — DEFINED, INACTIVE

**This Role is deliberately not staffed.** The CEO is currently the design source, so there is
no Product Designer seat: no binding, no generated agent, no status file, no Agent View node.

**Inactive is not absent.** The contract exists so the capability can be activated without
being reinvented, and so work that depends on design has a named owner to point at rather than
an assumption. Do not activate it because a ticket needs design — activate it when the CEO
stops being the design source.

## WHAT THIS ROLE IS

Product design as a **shared capability**: the design itself — flows, screens, states,
interaction and visual decisions — before anything is built.

## THE BOUNDARY

| | Product Designer | UX Engineer | CXO |
|---|---|---|---|
| Produces | the design | the built result matching it | the standard both are held to |
| Question | "what should this be?" | "does this match it?" | "does this meet our standard, our logic, our goals?" |

## GOVERNANCE

Under **`cxo`** experience governance, alongside UX Engineer and Content.

## ACTIVATION

Activation is a CEO decision, not a workload trigger. It requires a binding with
`role: product-designer` and a regeneration; nothing in this contract changes at that point.
