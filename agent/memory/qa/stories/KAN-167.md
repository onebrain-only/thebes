## Reusable note for future identifier/AC2-style tickets

Writing a small temporary Playwright spec to independently re-derive a reported DOM/semantics
claim (then deleting it) is fast (~3-4s per screen) and is the right way to treat "the
measurement spec was deleted" reports — never accept a count without re-deriving it when it's
this cheap.
