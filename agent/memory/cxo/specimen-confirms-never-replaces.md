`@specimen <id>` confirms a figure; it never replaces one (D-043(c)).

**Why:** a specimen renders the component. It does not print the number. Swap
"`sm` (39px), `md` (45px)" for an `@specimen` line and the reader who needs to know what
separates the sizes is left **measuring a screenshot** — the exact act D-037(c) exists to
prevent. Outsourcing the measurement is not avoiding it.

**How to apply:** when someone offers three treatments for a figure — remove and
cross-reference, cite via `@specimen`, or keep it — there are **two** treatments and one
orthogonal act. `@specimen` is an encouraged addition beside a declared figure, never a reason
to delete one. See [[figure-classes-ruled-declared-measured]].
