# Thebes domain language

- **Employee**: a durable organizational identity represented by a Seat and governed by a Role. Every employee can plan, perform Role Work, self-review, and learn within the current assignment, Role, and authority.
- **Seat**: one employee identity. A Seat is not a provider session, model, or tool configuration.
- **Role**: the employee's continuing accountability, decision authority, routine scope, and conflict boundaries. A Role is not a list of model capabilities.
- **Provider**: a replaceable execution mechanism used by an employee. Provider choice never changes the employee's Role or authority.
- **Work**: an outcome-producing act inside an employee's assignment and Role. A ticket, decision, delegation, investigation, test, design, release, or code change can each be Work; Work does not mean code by default.
- **Role Work**: the role-specific output or state change an employee is responsible for producing. Writing code is Role Work only when the assignment and Role call for code.
- **Code Implementation**: the creation or modification of product or infrastructure code. It is one possible kind of Role Work, not a synonym for Work or execution.
- **Accountability**: the continuing obligation to ensure an outcome is correct. Exactly one Role is accountable for a decision class.
- **Responsibility**: the obligation to perform a particular assigned act. Responsibility may be delegated; accountability remains with its owner.
- **Authority**: the right to make a bounded decision. Authority comes from a Role, an approved policy, or an explicit task-local delegation.
- **Decision owner**: the one accountable Role for a decision class. Other Roles may be consulted or informed but do not form a serial approval chain.
- **Owner decision**: a decision reserved to the CEO: company direction, investment envelope, legal or external commitment, or the constitution of authority itself.
- **Delegation**: a recorded transfer of responsibility for a bounded outcome from an authorized delegator to an eligible employee. Issuing it is itself Role Work.
- **Work cycle**: plan the assigned Role Work, perform it, self-review its role-specific output, then learn and adapt.
- **Self-review**: the employee's mandatory check of its own role-specific output, authority, evidence, and Definition of Done before reporting completion. It is not automatically code review or independent acceptance.
- **Independent review**: a separate check required only by explicit risk or conflict policy, never merely because Work was performed.
- **Definition of Done**: the completion obligations implied by an assignment, including relevant tests, commit, integration, and lifecycle update. Implied actions do not require repeated approval.
- **Learning candidate**: evidence-backed advice that has no authority by itself.
- **Adopted learning**: a decision-owned lesson with either a behavioral hook or an explicit informational classification.
- **Temporal scope**: the boundary of a direction or decision: invocation, task, session, project, product, organization, or until a named condition.
