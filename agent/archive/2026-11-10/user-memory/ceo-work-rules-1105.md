CEO 2026-11-05 (msg-20261105060822-79366836), after "merge everything to Alpha now":
- No folder per task. Plans go in dabbler-docs.
- Design is read from the Claude Design link, never from PNGs.
- Live test comes before screenshots.
- One branch per team.

**Why:** per-task folders and PNG designs were piling up; PNG designs drift from the real design; screenshots without a live test proved nothing.
**How to apply:** put these in every brief (ALLOWED PATHS, DECISIONS MADE, CHECKS) and in review instructions to Edfu. Related: [[design-link-means-reread]], [[designs-from-claude-design]], [[ceo-tests-localhost-first]].
