CEO 2026-10-15: we were in a loop where every story spawned more stories. For the current Messaging work (KAN-494 launch: KAN-546..563 and follow-ups), the PO and teams must NOT create tickets. When new work is found, I tell the CEO what ticket is needed (summary, capability, what it does, what it blocks) and he creates it through the Codex gate. Not a system rule; do not propose adding it to maintenance.

**Why:** scope creep from self-generated tickets.
**How to apply:** never brief the PO to create tickets during this work; PO may still edit links/status/comments. When a team reports a gap, give the CEO a ready ticket draft instead.
